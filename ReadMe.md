- The Good Cause

-- A community driven project to unmask corruption & malpractices in the bureaucracy & governments

-----------------------------------------

With modernsiation, & technical advancements, it has become easy to connect & keep various process transparent.
But, the "civil servants" have failed to deliver such transparency which often leads to corruption & malpractices.
Transparency in the matter of public administration is of an utmost importance whic safeguards public interests as well as creates a respectable environment with a tinch of harmony.
In recent years, the powers have been totally diverted towards buraeucracy which has made them unaccountable & unsanswerable to the public, by choice.
A civil servant's duty is to serve in people's interests, not in personal interests.
It is high time, now that we the Public should hold those acting as civil servants accountable.
